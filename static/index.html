NeuroSense
BIDIRECTIONAL GRU · 6-CLASS INFERENCE

Understand the emotion behind the words.

NeuroSense reads a sentence, runs it through a trained Bidirectional GRU network, and returns a full probability distribution across six emotions — with the confidence behind every call.

How it works

Five stages take raw text from your keyboard to a calibrated emotional signal.

1

Text Input

You write a sentence, in your own words.

2

NLP Processing

Cleaning, tokenizing, padding to length 50.

3

BiGRU Inference

Two bidirectional GRU layers read the sequence both ways.

4

Emotion Probability

A softmax layer scores all six emotions.

5

Insight Visualization

Results rendered as gauges, bars, and a radar.
